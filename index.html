      Notation reference

      Structure: Cast on 20 sts · Row 1 (RS): … · Rnd 3: … · Rows 3-10: repeat rows 1-2 · Repeat rows 1-2 until piece measures 10 cm, ending with a WS row · Join in the round · Knit 4 rows · Work in stockinette for 6 rows · Change to B · Bind off.

      Stitches: k, p, k2tog, ssk, k3tog, sk2p, s2kp/cdd, yo, kfb, pfb, m1, m1l, m1r, sl1 (wyif/wyib), psso, tbl, pm, sm, rm, w&t, turn, bo N, co N.

      Repeats: *k2, p2; rep from * to end · rep from * to last 3 sts · (k1, yo) 3 times · [k2tog] twice · k to end · p to last 2 sts · k to marker · k to 2 sts before marker.

      Sizes: Sizes: S (M, L) then Cast on 40 (44, 48) sts or k12 (14, 16). Pick the size in Yarn & gauge.
